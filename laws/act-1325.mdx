**Status:** Active

**Date of Publication:** 18th January, 1940

**Act No:** BENGAL REGULATION iii OF 1939

WHEREAS it is expedient to apply the 1[***] [Public Gambling Act, 1867](/laws/act-16 "Act 16") , to the whole of the Chittagong Hill- tracts;

It is hereby enacted as follows:-

### Short title

**1.** This Regulation may be called the Chittagong Hill- tracts Laws Regulation, 1939.

### Applications of Bengal Act II of 1867 to the Chittagong Hill- tracts

**2.** The [^1] [***] [Public Gambling Act, 1867](/laws/act-16 "Act 16") , shall apply to the whole of the Chittagong Hill-tracts.

* * *


### Footnotes

- [^1]: The word "Bengal" was omitted by Article 6 of the [Bangladesh (Adaptation of Existing Laws) Order, 1972](/laws/act-388 "Act 388") (Presidents Order No. 48 of 1972).
